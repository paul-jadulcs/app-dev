# My Favorite Movies

## 1. **Interstellar**
The movie is set in a near future where Earth is becoming uninhabitable due to environmental disaster, crop blight, and dust storms. The story follows ex-NASA pilot Cooper as he leaves his family behind on a mission to save mankind by finding a new home among the stars, exploring themes of love, sacrifice, and survival. 

- **Genre:** Sci-fi/Adventure
- **Screen Time:** 2 hours and 49 minutes

## 2. **Girl, Boy, Bakla, Tomboy**

Quadruplets are split into pairs and raised apart by their parents. They meet as adults by accident and have a tough choice to make when one needs a liver transplant.

- **Screen Time:** 1 hour and 43 minutes
